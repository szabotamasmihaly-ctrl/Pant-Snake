Sensor snake · hidden fill sensor

The snake knows when it is full

Twelve coils sit inside the belly wall, a thin cable runs along the keel, and a sealed pod under the fifth segment from the tail sends the level by radio. From the street you see only the snake. The pod opens only with your magnetic key.

Loading the snake

Every part, in one line

in the belly wall12 coil tabsFlat copper spirals pressed into pockets 1.2 mm from the cans. Cans roll over them but never touch them.
whereSpread along the bodyTwo in each of the top three segments, one in every segment below. Spread out, bottles cannot hide all of them.
along the bellyKeel, cable and coversA keel 48 mm wide and 7 mm deep carries the cable in a channel. 2 mm cover strips close it and are trapped by the next segment.
in the keel3 reader chips (TI LDC1614)Each reads four coils. Metal changes a coil's inductance, so a can over it shows up and a plastic bottle does not.
in the podRAK3172 radio + ER14505 batteryLying side by side on a printed tray, so the pod is only 19.5 mm deep. About ten years on one battery.
the lockTwo spring bolts, one keyMagnets in your key draw the bolts 4.5 mm open. Pulling or prising only presses the hooks harder onto the bolts.

Built on the light-pole snake v2, 9-segment standard build (mouth 1.67 m, 24 cans). Figures from the sensor snake README and physics notes and the hidden-sensor plan, 19 Oct 2026. Estimates; nothing is bench-tested yet. The first test is one coil reading a can through 1.2 mm of print, then radio range from a full snake beside a steel pole.
